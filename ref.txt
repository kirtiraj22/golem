## 1. Product context

Golem allows companies and creators to evaluate images through human participation.

The workflow:

1. A company connects its Solana wallet and creates a campaign.
2. It uploads a collection of images and specifies the campaign budget and voting rules.
3. It deposits the campaign budget in native SOL into an on-chain escrow.
4. Participants connect their wallets and classify each image as either `AI_GENERATED` or `HUMAN_CREATED`.
5. Each participant can vote only once per image.
6. Once voting ends, the on-chain program determines the majority classification for each image.
7. Participants whose votes match the majority become eligible to claim an equal share of that image's reward pool.
8. After the claim period expires, the company can recover all remaining funds.

**Important product distinction:** Golem measures human consensus, not objectively verified AI-detection accuracy. A majority vote is the winning classification; it must never be represented as verified ground truth.

There is no AI model, AI inference service, AI detection API, or AI-generated prediction functionality in this implementation. Humans perform the classification.

## 6. Consensus and reward calculations

Implement these rules exactly.

Each asset is evaluated independently.

Final result:

- If `total_votes < minimum_votes`: `INSUFFICIENT_VOTES`.
- If AI votes exceed human votes: `AI_GENERATED`.
- If human votes exceed AI votes: `HUMAN_CREATED`.
- If both counts are equal: `TIE`.

Reward allocation:

- Divide the campaign budget equally among the declared number of assets.
- Each asset receives `floor(total_budget / asset_count)` lamports as its reward pool.
- For a finalized asset, divide its reward pool equally among participants who voted for the winning classification.
- Each participant receives `floor(asset_reward_pool / winning_vote_count)` lamports.
- Participants who voted against the majority receive nothing.
- Tied or insufficient-vote assets have no reward claims.
- All integer-division remainders remain in escrow and are refunded to the company after the claim deadline.

Use integer lamports and safe checked arithmetic throughout. Never use floating-point calculations for money.

The program must never pay more than the declared campaign budget.

**Escrow safety is critical:**
- Preserve the Campaign PDA's rent-exempt minimum.
- Only transfer spendable escrow funds.
- Prevent unauthorized withdrawals.
- Prevent underflow, overflow, duplicate settlement, and overpayment.
- Ensure all state changes and transfers occur atomically.
- Validate every PDA, signer, owner, and account relationship.
